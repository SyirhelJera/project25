Navbar Tabs
Drag a tab by its handle to change the order it appears in the sidebar, and hit Shown/Hidden to keep a tab out of the navbar. Hiding only removes the tab from the sidebar and the mobile switcher — nothing in it is deleted, so switching it back on brings everything with it. Settings always stays put.
Tab Icons
Show or hide the little logo next to each tab in the navbar. On mobile the tab strip is icon-only, so hiding them switches it to text labels instead.

Trend Comparison
What the ▲▼ arrows next to Net Worth and Fitness Level measure against — on the profile card and on the Goals board. "Since the last entry" compares the two most recent readings, whenever they happened; the others reach back a fixed distance and compare against the closest reading on or before that date. If nothing was recorded that far back, the oldest reading you have is used instead.
