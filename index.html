Trend comparison
What the ▲▼ arrows next to Net Worth and Fitness Level measure against — on the profile card and on the Goals board. "The last entry" compares the two most recent readings, whenever they happened. "Start of this week/month" answers how far you've moved so far this week or month; the day counts reach back a fixed distance instead. Either way it compares against the closest reading on or before that date, and if nothing was recorded that far back, the oldest reading you have is used. On a phone, tap a figure on the Goals board to read the full comparison.
Net worth display currency

Calendar reminder
Bubbles in the top corner showing what's next on your Google Calendar, as long as it's within the coming week — all-day events included. They appear once each time you open the app. Click one to jump to the Calendar pane, ✕ to clear just that card, or click anywhere else to clear them all — either way it only lasts until the next time you open the app, so nothing is ever silenced for good. Countdowns show a ⏳ and open the Countdowns pane instead. Turning the reminder off also stops the app reading your calendar on load.
HenrikDev API key
Rank and match-history lookups on the Valorant tab use the unofficial HenrikDev API, which needs a free key — join https://discord.com/invite/X3GaVkX2YN, then generate one on the api.henrikdev.xyz dashboard.
Valorant Points prices
Powers the top-up calculator: click any VP-priced item on the Shop Tracker and it works out what you're short and which packages cover it most cheaply. Leave a tier blank if it isn't sold in your region — it won't be suggested. With no prices at all the calculator still runs, but optimises for the least wasted VP rather than the lowest cost.
Third-party offers
A discounted top-up shop selling the same VP for less. Offers are grouped by seller — add every tier a shop sells under one name, and its checkbox takes that whole seller out of the calculator without deleting anything. All fields stay editable, since a shop's prices change. Each offer is weighed against the official tiers, and when one wins the calculator also shows what the official-only route would have cost.
Local helper
Connects to scripts/valorant-local-server.mjs running on this machine so you can trigger a store check, save Riot account sessions, or delete one here instead of the terminal (see README.md). Pick which saved account to act on from the dropdown under the store on the Valorant tab.
